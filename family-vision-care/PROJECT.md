# Family Vision Care of Ponca City — Gemini in Gmail for Patient Communication

## Overview
Family Vision Care of Ponca City uses **Gemini in Gmail** to:
- Easily explain medical terms in patient emails
- Improve accessibility of patient communication

## Core Idea
A small private optometry practice leverages built-in Gemini AI features in Gmail / Google Workspace to make patient communication clearer, faster, and more accessible — without needing a custom-built AI product.

## Tech Stack
- **Google Workspace** (Gmail + Docs)
- **Gemini in Gmail** ("Help me write", smart compose, summarization)
- Optional: Gemini for translating into Spanish for bilingual patients

## Key Features
1. **Medical jargon simplification** — Gemini rewrites clinical terms ("posterior vitreous detachment") in plain language.
2. **Drafting assistance** — staff write patient emails faster using "Help me write".
3. **Accessibility improvements** — clearer language for elderly patients, ESL patients, low-literacy patients.
4. **Translation** — automatic language translation for diverse patient base.

## Target Users
- Small/medium optometry and primary care practices
- Patient-facing administrative staff
- Practices with limited IT budgets

## Project Plan (representative SMB rollout)
1. **Workspace setup** — enable Gemini for Workspace on practice domain.
2. **Staff training** — quick training on prompts: "rewrite this in plain language", "explain at a 6th-grade reading level".
3. **Templates** — shared response templates with placeholders Gemini fills in.
4. **Quality review** — clinical staff sign off on AI-drafted patient communications.
5. **Patient feedback loop** — survey patients on clarity.

## Business Value
- Low-cost AI adoption for small practices
- Reduces time per patient email
- Improves patient understanding and adherence
- Better experience for non-English-speaking and elderly patients
- Showcases that AI in healthcare doesn't require massive infrastructure
